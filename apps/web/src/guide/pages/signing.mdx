# Signing and sealing <Plan tier="free" />

Signing proves you wrote it: a changed copy can no longer claim to be yours, and once the key is claimed by your account the app names you beside it. Open **Publish** in the Designer. It reviews **Identifier and version**, **License** and **Signature**, then **Sign and seal** makes or takes a key, claims it and downloads the signed pack. The private half stays in the page while it is open and is never sent anywhere; only the public half is shared when you claim it.

The same section holds **Documents**, which previews and downloads the generated documents. **Share** downloads the editable pack or copies a link containing it. Signing can bundle the documents with the release instead.

## A copy for a buyer

**Seal a copy for a buyer** stamps a copy with their name, seals it so it needs a license key to open, and gives you the key to send them. That is the whole of [selling from your own hands](#guide/selling-yourself); the app never sees the sale.

## Publishing <Plan tier="publisher" />

Listing a pack in the marketplace is managed under **Profile → Publishing**, after the pack itself is ready in the Designer's **Publish** section. See [Through the marketplace](#guide/selling-marketplace).

The same keys and the same sealing are on [the command line](#guide/cli-sign), for a script or a build server.

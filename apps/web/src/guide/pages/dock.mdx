# A dock for the controls

OBS can keep a signed-in web page docked beside the preview: **View** → **Docks** → **Custom Browser Docks**. Give it a name ("Runlog") and, from the run's Stream panel, **Copy dock address**: it is the run's remote alone on a page (the next move, the last result, undo). **Apply**, and sign in inside the dock, once; OBS keeps that sign-in between sessions, and the address survives the trip through the sign-in service and back.

## A second device at your table

The dock is a second device, so the run must be on it: on the machine you play on it already is, and on another it arrives through sync once you are signed in there. A move in the dock appears on your other screens shortly afterward.

The dock shows controls without sending dice or ticker lines to the stream. The widget beside it displays those events.

## The whole app in a dock

For the whole app rather than the remote, use the plain address of the app, `https://runlog.scrthq.com/play`, and open the run there.

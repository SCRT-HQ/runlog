# Playing together

Signing in adds your runs on every device, other people in your runs, and a run one person moderates for a room. A stream or a Discord server has parts of its own.

## On every device <Plan tier="free" />

Sign in; **Sync on this device** in the menu is on by default and can be turned off per device. Your runs go to your account and come back to your other devices; the one you touched last is what **Continue where you left off** opens.

## What the rest of this part covers

- [Inviting someone](#guide/inviting) into a run as a player or a watcher, and a friend to Runlog itself.
- [Moderated play](#guide/moderated): one person at the app, everyone else on a roster, points on a grid.
- [A live link](#guide/live-link): an address anyone opens to watch, no account needed.
- [Races across devices](#guide/races): the same seeded mode, each on their own device, one leaderboard.

<Note>Where plans are switched on, the badges say what needs Plus: hosting a table. Where they are not, in the preview or a copy you run yourself, everything here is open to everyone. Your profile says which it is, under Plan.</Note>

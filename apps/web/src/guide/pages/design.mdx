# Designing a pack

A pack is the whole game: its words, its tables, its flow, its modes. The app plays any pack that loads. Press **Create** in the app bar, or in the menu on a phone, to open the Designer without leaving the browser. Its address is `#create`.

The six sections are **Overview**, **Tables**, **Flow**, **Modes**, **Test** and **Publish**. Open them in any order; they are parts of one draft, not steps that have to be completed in sequence. The draft is saved on this device after every edit. **Try it** opens a test run that is not saved.

## Starting

- **Start from a pack…** offers packs from your library and the marketplace as starting points. A license that allows changed copies makes a new pack of your own with the original credited in its notice. License-ineligible packs stay listed with the reason shown. Sealed packs are not offered.
- **Open a file…** loads a `.yaml` or `.json` you wrote elsewhere.
- Or start empty and move among [the sections](#guide/sections) as the pack takes shape.

Those choices are under **Draft**. Starting again, opening a file or starting from another pack replaces the current draft, so the Designer asks first.

## The format itself

The Designer writes the same YAML [the command line](#guide/cli) takes. The authoring guide walks through a complete pack and the reference names every field; both are under [Reference](#guide/reference).

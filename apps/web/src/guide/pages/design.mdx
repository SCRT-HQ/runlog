# Designing a pack

A pack is the whole game: its words, its tables, its flow, its modes. The app plays any pack that loads, and the **Designer** (the button in the header, or `#create` in the address) writes one without leaving the browser.

The sections run down the left in the order a pack is read; **Problems**, **Sign** and **Documents** sit on the right. Every field carries the same help the file format's reference does.

## Starting

- **Start from a pack…** opens any pack in your library or the marketplace whose license allows changed copies, as a new pack of your own with the original credited in its notice. Packs under a no-derivatives license, and sealed packs, are refused with the reason shown.
- **Open a file…** loads a `.yaml` or `.json` you wrote elsewhere.
- Or start empty and fill in [the sections](#guide/sections) in order.

## The format itself

The Designer writes the same YAML [the command line](#guide/cli) takes. The authoring guide walks through a complete pack and the reference names every field; both are under [Reference](#guide/reference).

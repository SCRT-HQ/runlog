# The board

The side column is the run so far. Each thing you made is a row with its states as chips; each tally and track is a line; a hand of cards, where the pack deals one, sits with them; and the people at the table are at the bottom.

## Correcting it

When something was recorded wrong, fix it there: take a state off with its ×, put one on from **mark…**, nudge a tally with − and +. Every correction goes into the log after a marker that says so, and can be [undone](#guide/undo).

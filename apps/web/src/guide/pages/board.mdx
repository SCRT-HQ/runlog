# The board

The side column shows the run's current state. Each item you made has a row with its states shown as chips. Tallies and tracks appear below, along with any cards the pack deals. The people at the table appear at the bottom.

## Correcting it

When something was recorded wrong, fix it there: take a state off with its ×, put one on from **mark…**, nudge a tally with − and +. Every correction goes into the log after a marker that says so, and can be [undone](#guide/undo).

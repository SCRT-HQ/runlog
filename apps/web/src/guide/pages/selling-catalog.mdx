# Through the marketplace <Plan tier="publisher" />

Become a publisher on your profile, set up payouts with Stripe, upload the signed pack, and list it at a price. The marketplace does the rest:

- The card, with your name, the pack's summary, and **Buy**.
- The sale, on your own Stripe account: the buyer pays you directly and you are on the receipt.
- A copy sealed for that buyer alone, under a fresh key, delivered at once and kept in their account.
- The ledger on your profile: what sold, to whom, for how much; send a lost key again; revoke one.
- People: under **People in** your publisher, an admin invites others by email. A member uploads, lists and sees the ledger; an admin also invites and removes people; payouts stay with the founder.

## From version control

With `RUNLOG_API_KEY` set, `npx @scrthq/runlog release my-pack.yaml --price 3.00` uploads and lists in one command; a later release without a price keeps the one it has. The `marketplace-release` workflow in the repository's `examples/github-actions` runs it when a release is published; see [Publishing and releasing](#guide/cli-publish).

## The fee

The marketplace takes 5% of each sale, or nothing with the hosted-licensing subscription. Stripe's own fees are separate and yours.

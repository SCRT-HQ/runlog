# Taking it back

Everything can be exported at any time from the library, run by run or pack by pack.

## Deleting the account

Deleting your account from your profile deletes what the server holds at once: runs, packs, keys, links to other accounts, the people you played with, and the servers you claimed. The copies on your devices stay. A purchase receipt, when the marketplace sells packs, is kept as long as tax law requires.

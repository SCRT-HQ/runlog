# Where the app runs

| Copy | Sign-in and sync | Everything else |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The hosted copy, `runlog.scrthq.com` | Yes | Yes |
| A file on disk, or `npx @scrthq/runlog serve` | No | Yes, offline included |
| The GitHub Pages copy | No | Yes, offline included |

Your packs and runs stay in the browser they were made in unless you turn sync on. [Running your own copy](#guide/own-copy) says how to put the app at an address of your own, with or without accounts.

# The header

The strip across the top is the same on every screen.

- **The pack switcher** in the middle names the pack you are in. Press it for your [library](#guide/library).
- **Rules** opens the pack's paper ([Documents](#guide/documents) says what each is) and, as one more tab, the structure the engine holds (every table, the flow, the modes).
- **Designer** opens the [Designer](#guide/design), where you write a pack of your own.
- **Guide** opens these pages.
- **Menu** holds your theme and, when you are signed in, sync and your profile.

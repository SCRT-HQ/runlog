# The header

The strip across the top is the same on every screen.

- **The pack switcher** in the middle names the pack you are in. Press it for your [library](#guide/library): every pack you have, with its runs beneath.
- **Rules** opens the pack's paper: the rulebook, the quick start, the reference card, the run log sheet, the summary, and, as one more tab, the structure the engine holds: every table, the flow, the modes. [Documents](#guide/documents) says what each is.
- **Designer** opens the [Designer](#guide/design), where you write a pack of your own.
- **Guide** opens these pages.
- **Menu** holds your theme and, when you are signed in, sync and your profile.

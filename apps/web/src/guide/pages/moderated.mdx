# Moderated play <Plan tier="plus" />

A **moderated** mode is for players who cannot hold the device because they are in the match or on stream: one person is at the app and everyone else is on a roster of names, no accounts needed.

## How it plays

- Each result the round draws lands on everyone.
- The results that carry **points** are the challenges. **Winners** is a grid, a row per challenge and a column per contestant; press a cell when someone finishes. Under "first to finish" a row holds one winner and pressing another cell moves the award; under "everyone finishes" each cell is its own award, the first marked ★ for the bonus.
- The **scoreboard** keeps standings, ties sharing a place. The moderator can add a late arrival, drop someone, or mark a contestant from the row (spared from a curse, or out of this region).
- Watchers see the grid and the scoreboard and press nothing.

Built this way: **Rocket League: Showdown**, **Elden Ring: Trial**, and **Forfeits**, a penalty wheel that names no game and lands on the whole roster.

## On a stream

The scoreboard is one of the [widgets](#guide/streaming), so the standings can sit on the scene while the moderator awards from the app.

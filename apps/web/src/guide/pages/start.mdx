# Getting started

Runlog turns something you already do — a day of work, a practice session, a match, a firing — into a run: a series of rounds where the dice hand you a twist, you do the thing, and you log what happened. The rules live in a **pack**. The app plays any pack, and comes with eleven.

Nothing here needs an account. Sign in only when you want your runs on more than one device or want to play with other people.

## The first five minutes

<Steps>
  <li>Open the app. The first time, it gives you **Any Given Day**, a pack for anyone with things to do today.</li>
  <li>Press **Enter the day**. Pick a mode — a working day, one morning, a shared seed, or together — name the day if you like, and go.</li>
  <li>The left margin shows where you are in the round. The middle is the one thing to do next. The right is the board: what you have made, the tallies, and who is at the table.</li>
  <li>Roll when asked. Let the app roll for you with **Auto-roll**, or type what your own dice said.</li>
  <li>Do the thing. Tick the boxes that say you did. **Call it** to close the round.</li>
</Steps>

## The header

- **The pack switcher** in the middle names the pack you are in. Press it for your library: every pack you have, with its runs beneath.
- **Rules** opens the pack's paper: the rulebook, the quick start, the reference card, the run log sheet, the summary, and, as one more tab, the structure the engine holds: every table, the flow, the modes.
- **Designer** opens the Designer, where you write a pack of your own.
- **Guide** opens these pages.
- **Menu** holds your theme and — when you are signed in — sync and your profile.

## Where the app runs

The same app runs at the hosted address, from a file on disk, and from the public GitHub Pages copy. On disk and on Pages there is no sign-in and no sync; everything else works, offline included. Your packs and runs stay in the browser they were made in unless you turn sync on.

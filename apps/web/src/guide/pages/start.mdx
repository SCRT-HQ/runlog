# Getting started

Runlog adds dice-driven challenges to an activity, such as a day of work, a practice session, a match, or a firing. A run consists of rounds: roll, follow the result, and record what happened. A **pack** defines the rules. The app includes several packs and can play others.

No account is needed until you want your runs on more than one device or want to play with other people.

## The first five minutes

<Steps>
  <li>Open the app. The first time, it gives you **Forfeits**, a penalty wheel that works with whatever you are playing.</li>
  <li>Press **Enter the day**. Pick a mode (a working day, one morning, a shared seed, or together); name the day if you like, and go.</li>
  <li>
    The left column shows your place in the round, the middle shows the next action, and the right shows the board: what you have made, the
    tallies, who is at the table.
  </li>
  <li>Roll when prompted. Use **Auto-roll** or enter the result of your own dice.</li>
  <li>Complete the action, tick the completed items, and press **Call it** to close the round.</li>
</Steps>

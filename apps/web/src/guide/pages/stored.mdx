# What the server holds

Signed in, the server holds:

- **Your runs**, every move, so your other devices and the people you invite see the same run.
- **Your packs**, the ones you switched sync on for. A pack marked as not shareable is still stored for you alone and shown to nobody else.
- **Accounts you linked**, under Social: for Discord, the user id and the name it showed when you ran `/link`, nothing else of Discord's. Unlink there whenever you like.
- **License keys** for sealed packs, so they follow you.
- **Your purchases** from the catalog: the receipt, the key, and the sealed copy, so a device you sign in on fetches what you bought on the next sync, and the catalog shows it as yours.
- **Invitations**: the address you invited, for which run, and whether it was used. One email goes to that address and nothing else ever will.
- **People you have played with**, so the next invitation is a name.
- **The name you are shown as.** Your account's name comes from sign-in; under **Shown as** on your profile you can choose another, and that is what a table, a race, a claim and a reaction show. Your address is shown to nobody but you, and to a run's owner only when they invite it.
- **Your profile**: when the account was first and last seen, the run you touched last, and which version of the terms you accepted.
- **Servers you claimed** for the Runlog bot, with the packs you put in each one's vault, and the runs it hosted there.

<Note>The hosted copy's privacy policy, linked from the foot of every page, says the same in the words a policy needs, and names who runs it and how to reach them.</Note>

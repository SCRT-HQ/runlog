# The catalog

**Get more packs** in the library opens the catalog: the packs that come with the app and what people have published, laid out as cards. A free one goes straight to your packs; a priced one is bought from its publisher and arrives sealed, with a key kept in your account.

## Finding one

The sidebar narrows them: search, category, tags, and **how it plays** (solo, together, moderated, seeded, endless, timers, cards, journal, reaches back). All of that is read from the packs themselves, so a filter cannot disagree with the rules.

## What a card says

Each card says what a pack **needs** in the world (the game and a system to run it, a wheel, a kitchen) and what it is **better with**. **Docs** opens the pack's paper in the side drawer, on the summary: the shape of the game, its tables by name, its modes, without giving its rules away; the rulebook, the quick start and the rest are the drawer's tabs. **Add to my packs** puts it in the library.

# Your packs and the catalog

Press the pack switcher in the header for your **library**: every pack you have, newest played first, with its runs beneath it. Continue a run, start another without disturbing the one that is open, forget a run, or forget a pack and its runs together.

## The catalog

**Get more packs** opens the catalog: the packs that come with the app and what people have published, laid out as cards. A free one goes straight to your packs; a priced one is bought from its publisher and arrives sealed, with a key kept in your account. The sidebar narrows them — search, category, tags, and **how it plays**: solo, together, moderated, seeded, endless, timers, cards, journal, reaches back. All of that is read from the packs themselves, so a filter cannot disagree with the rules.

<Screenshot src="catalog.png" alt="The catalog as a grid of cards with a filter sidebar." caption="The catalog. Cards say what a pack needs; About opens its summary." />

Each card says what a pack **needs** in the world — the game and a system to run it, a wheel, a kitchen — and what it is **better with**. **About** opens the pack's summary: the shape of the game, its tables by name, its modes, without giving its rules away. **Add to my packs** puts it in the library.

## Packs from elsewhere

**Load a pack from a file** takes a `.yaml`, `.json` or a sealed `.rlpack`. A sealed pack asks for its license key once and remembers it. A pack marked private by its license stays on this device and never syncs its text.

## Keeping packs in sync

Signed in, your runs sync to your account and back to your other devices on their own. A catalog pack's text is public and syncs with them. A pack you loaded from a file stays here unless you tick **keep this pack in sync** on its row.

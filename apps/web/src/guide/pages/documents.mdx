# Documents

Runlog writes a game's paper from the pack, so it cannot disagree with the rules:

- **Rulebook**: everything in reading order; what you need, the terms, a unit step by step, the modes, every table with every entry, the states, tallies, moves and endings.
- **Quick start**: enough to play the first time, with a pointer to the reference card for the tables.
- **Reference card**: every table in columns, plus a list of the things people forget.
- **Run log sheet**: a printable form to write a run on by hand.
- **Summary**: what the catalog shows, the shape of the game without its rules.

## Where to get them

Press **Documents** wherever a pack is:

- on the **Rules** page of the pack you are in,
- on a pack's row in your **library**,
- under **About** on a catalog card,
- in the **Designer**, for the pack you are writing, with a preview.

Each opens in a drawer beside what you were doing, with the other documents a press away; **Print** opens it as its own page for paper or a PDF, the reference card in columns and the run log as a sheet. The files themselves (PDF, HTML and Markdown, to keep or to send) are made in the Designer. The PDF is laid out in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

The same documents come from the terminal with [`docs`](#guide/cli-docs), for a build that writes the paper beside the pack.

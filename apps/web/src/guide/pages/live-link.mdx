# A live link <Plan tier="plus" />

Under **People at the table**, **Share a live link** gives you an address anyone can open, with no account, to watch the run as it happens: the log, the board, the clocks, the scoreboard. **Stop sharing** kills the link.

## What a watcher sees

The page tells the current stage phase by phase, with what each phase produced under it; a table a roll set off is named with the piece it hit. **All stages** tells the whole run that way, newest first, in place of the log. A watcher can send a reaction to the table, and, signed in, take a seat so the run follows them into their own library as a watcher.

What a viewer sees of the pack follows its license: a pack whose text may travel is shown whole, its paper a press away; a pack marked not for redistribution shows the state and the log by reference, never the rules.

A watcher by link is not a member: they cannot move, and they are not in the run's people.

## The same link feeds a stream

The token in the link is what the [stream widgets](#guide/streaming) and [a tool of your own](#guide/stream-tools) read the run with. Whoever has it can watch, so keep it out of anything you publish; **Stop sharing** kills it, and every address that carried it.

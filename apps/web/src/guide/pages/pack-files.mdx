# Packs from a file

**Load a pack from a file** in the library takes a `.yaml`, `.json` or a sealed `.rlpack`. A sealed pack asks for its license key once and remembers it. A pack marked private by its license stays on this device and never syncs its text.

## A newer file of the same pack

A pack is known by its id, so a file with the id of a pack already here is that pack, edited. **Replace from a file** on the pack's row takes it and keeps the runs; **Load a pack from a file** with the same file does the same, and opens it. Whether the pack travels to your account stays as you set it, and the new text goes up on the next sync. A sealed copy is not replaced this way: its publisher's update comes through the catalog.

## Keeping packs in sync

Signed in, your runs sync to your account and back to your other devices on their own. A catalog pack's text is public and syncs with them. A pack you loaded from a file stays here unless you tick **keep this pack in sync** on its row.

# Packs from a file

**Load a pack from a file** in the library takes a `.yaml`, `.json` or a sealed `.rlpack`. A sealed pack asks for its license key once and remembers it. A pack marked private by its license stays on this device and never syncs its text.

## A newer file of the same pack

A pack is known by its id, so a file with the id of a pack already here is that pack, edited. **Replace from a file** on the pack's row takes it and keeps the runs; **Load a pack from a file** does the same and opens it. Sync stays as you set it, and the new text goes up on the next sync. A sealed copy is updated by its publisher through the marketplace instead.

## Keeping packs in sync

Signed in, your runs sync to your account and your other devices. A marketplace pack's text is public and syncs with them. A pack loaded from a file stays here unless you tick **keep this pack in sync** on its row.

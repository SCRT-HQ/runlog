# The marketplace

**Get more packs** under **Add and discover** opens the marketplace: the packs that come with the app and what people have published, laid out as cards. **Add** puts a free pack in your packs. A priced pack offers **Buy** when buying is available and you are signed in.

## Finding one

Search and the first filters narrow by **Activity**, **Solo or group**, and **Free or owned**. Where the marketplace also carries setups, **Looking for** switches between them and packs. **More filters** holds how it plays, publishers and tags. The available choices come from the listings currently shown.

## What a card says

Each card links its title to a page for that pack. It shows the premise, activity, ways to play, price and ownership, plus what the pack **Needs** and what is **Optional** when those lists exist. **Read the summary** opens the pack's paper in the side drawer. A free pack may also offer the rulebook, quick start and other documents there. **Add** puts a free pack in [your packs](#guide/library).

The pack page repeats the action and, when the listing has them, shows **Modes**, **Documents**, **Stream Deck**, **Publisher** and **Version**.

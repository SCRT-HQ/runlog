## The kinds of step

- **Roll on a table.** Press the table's button. With **Auto-roll** on, the app rolls; off, a tray asks for what your dice said, with **Roll for me** if you would rather it did. The dice are thrown into a tray in three dimensions where the browser can draw them, landing on the value the engine already rolled; **Dice in three dimensions** under Alerts and sounds switches back to the flat tray. The result and its consequences show as a receipt until you **Carry on**.
- **Declare** what this unit is about, the piece, the task, the target, in your own words.
- **Do the thing.** The part the app cannot see. It shows you what the tables said and asks you to tick that you honored them. A box that shows a table you never rolled on is not shown; a box marked optional does not hold the step up.
- **Close the unit.** The pack's own word for it: Fire, Log, Leave the region. Anything still owed blocks it until settled.

## Owed, and moves

Some results reach forward in time, a note to keep, a consequence due when the unit closes, and appear under **Owed** until you settle them. Some packs offer **moves** between steps, like taking a break or breaking a piece on purpose; a move that *is* the unit's outcome closes the unit as well.

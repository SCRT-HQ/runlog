## On entering

Some packs speak when a unit begins, a welcome on the first and a word on each. It sits above the first step until that step is done.

## The kinds of step

- **Roll on a table.** Press the table's button. With **Auto-roll** on the app rolls; off, a tray asks what your dice said, or **Roll for me**. Where the browser can draw them, the dice land in three dimensions on the value the engine rolled; **Dice in three dimensions** under Alerts and sounds switches back to the flat tray. The result and its consequences show as a receipt until you **Carry on**.
- **Declare** what this unit is about (the piece, the task, the target) in your own words.
- **Do the thing.** The part the app cannot see: it shows what the tables said and asks you to tick that you honored them. A box for a table you did not roll on is hidden; a box marked optional does not hold the step up.
- **Close the unit.** The pack's own word for it (Fire, Log, Leave the region), or in some packs the work's own Done. It ends in a choice: **Next** opens the next unit; **Finish** ends the run, asking how where the pack offers more than one ending. Anything still owed blocks both until settled.

## Owed, and moves

Results that reach forward (a note to keep, a consequence due when the unit closes) sit under **Owed** until settled. Some packs offer **moves** between steps, like taking a break or breaking a piece on purpose; a move that *is* the unit's outcome closes it too.

# Playing a run

A run is one session of a pack. Each pack has its own words for the parts, a Firing of Stages, a Day of Blocks, a Trial of Regions, but the shape is always the same: a run is a series of **units**, each unit passes through the pack's **phases**, and each phase is a step or two.

## Beginning

The setup screen asks for what the mode needs and nothing else:

- **Mode.** Every pack has at least one. A mode sets the length, and may seed the dice, seat several players, or make the run a moderated race.
- **Seed.** A seeded mode rolls its own dice from the seed, so everyone who enters the same seed meets the same run.
- **Players or contestants.** A together mode seats people at one device; a moderated one takes a roster of names.
- **What you need.** Packs can say what they need: the game, a wheel, a kitchen. Untick anything optional you do not have, and the dice will not ask for it.
- **A name**, optional, for telling this run from the next.

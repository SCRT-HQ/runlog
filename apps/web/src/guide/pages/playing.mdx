# Playing a run

A run is one session of a pack. Each pack has its own words for the parts — a Firing of Stages, a Day of Blocks, a Trial of Regions — but the shape is always the same: a run is a series of **units**, each unit passes through the pack's **phases**, and each phase is a step or two.

## Beginning

The setup screen asks for what the mode needs and nothing else:

- **Mode.** Every pack has at least one. A mode sets the length, and may seed the dice, seat several players, or make the run a moderated race.
- **Seed.** A seeded mode rolls its own dice from the seed, so everyone who enters the same seed meets the same run.
- **Players or contestants.** A together mode seats people at one device; a moderated one takes a roster of names.
- **What you need.** Packs can say what they need — the game, a wheel, a kitchen. Untick anything optional you do not have, and the dice will not ask for it.
- **A name**, optional, for telling this run from the next.

## The round

Steps come in a few kinds:

- **Roll on a table.** Press the table's button. With **Auto-roll** on, the app rolls; off, a tray asks for what your dice said, with **Roll for me** if you would rather it did. The dice are thrown into a tray in three dimensions where the browser can draw them, landing on the value the engine already rolled; **Dice in three dimensions** under Alerts and sounds switches back to the flat tray. The result and its consequences show as a receipt until you **Carry on**.
- **Declare** what this unit is about — the piece, the task, the target — in your own words.
- **Do the thing.** The part the app cannot see. It shows you what the tables said and asks you to tick that you honored them. A box that shows a table you never rolled on is not shown; a box marked optional does not hold the step up.
- **Close the unit.** The pack's own word for it: Fire, Log, Leave the region. Anything still owed blocks it until settled.

Some results reach forward in time — a note to keep, a consequence due when the unit closes — and appear under **Owed** until you settle them. Some packs offer **moves** between steps, like taking a break or breaking a piece on purpose; a move that *is* the unit's outcome closes the unit as well.

## The board

The side column is the run so far. Each thing you made is a row with its states as chips; each tally and track is a line. When something was recorded wrong, fix it there: take a state off with its ×, put one on from **mark…**, nudge a tally with − and +. Every correction goes into the log after a marker that says so, and can be undone.

## Undo and the log

**Undo** takes back the last move. Nothing is erased: the log is append-only, and an undo is itself written down, which is what lets two devices share a run without either rewriting the other. The log at the bottom of the middle column is the run as it happened.

## Ending

When the mode allows it, the run offers its endings between units. Each is the pack's own — keep the set, break what you dislike, carry it out the door — and some are only open once you have earned them.

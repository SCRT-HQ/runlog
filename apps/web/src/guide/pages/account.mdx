# Your account and what is stored

Runlog keeps everything on your device first. This group of pages says what leaves it, when, and how to take it back.

## Without an account

Your packs, runs, settings and license keys live in the browser's own storage on this device. Nothing about you is sent anywhere; there are no analytics and no tracking. The only request beyond the app's own files is the catalog fetching its list of packs.

Clearing the browser's site data clears Runlog with it, so export what you care about first: any run and any pack can be saved as a file from the library. A second browser, or a second device, starts empty.

## With an account

Signing in adds a server, and [What the server holds](#guide/stored) lists everything it keeps. Sync is a switch per device: turn it off in the menu and this device stops sending and receiving; what is already on the server stays until you [delete it](#guide/taking-back).

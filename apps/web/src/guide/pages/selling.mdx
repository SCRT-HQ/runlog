# Selling your packs

There are two ways, and the line between them is who keeps the receipts.

- [**From your own hands**](#guide/selling-yourself): sign the pack, seal a copy for each buyer, send it however you sell. Free, no account needed, no share taken, and nothing given back.
- [**Through the marketplace**](#guide/selling-catalog): a card with your name, the sale on your own Stripe account, a copy sealed for each buyer by the marketplace, and a ledger.

## Mixing the two

A publisher can list one pack free and sell another outside the marketplace; a pack sealed by your own hands is not in the ledger, and one sold through the marketplace needs no sealing by you. Either way the pack is signed by you alone: the marketplace seals what you signed and never holds your signing key.

<Note>The publisher agreement, linked from the foot of the hosted copy, says the rest in the words an agreement needs: you are the seller, Stripe holds the money, and a sale is final once the copy has been opened, except where the law says otherwise.</Note>

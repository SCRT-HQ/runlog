# Plans and pricing

Runlog is free software. The app, the engine, the Designer, the command line and every built-in pack cost nothing and always will, wherever you run them. What costs money is what a server adds for you.

## Free <Plan tier="free" />

Everything you can do on one device, and your own devices together:

- Every pack, every mode, offline included.
- The Designer, documents, and the command line.
- Sync between your own devices, once you sign in.
- Playing together on one device, moderated play included.
- Buying packs from the catalog.

## Plus <Plan tier="plus" />

Hosting a table: other people in your runs, on their own devices.

- Invite people into a run as players or watchers.
- Races across devices, with a live leaderboard.
- Pop-out widgets for a stream: the scoreboard, the clock, the race, on pages of their own.
- A live link: anyone watches a run as it happens, with no account.

## Servers <Plan tier="server" />

Runlog in a Discord server of yours. Add the Runlog bot, claim the server for your account from your profile, and put packs from your own shelf in its vault; people with the host role you choose start runs there, and the bot plays them in a thread, on the packs you chose, for everyone in the server. The packs never leave the vault: members see the lines the dice draw, not the pack.

## Publisher <Plan tier="publisher" />

Listing packs in the catalog, free or for sale, paid through your own Stripe account, with a ledger of what sold: reissue a buyer's key, revoke one. A sale carries a small fee, or none with the hosted-licensing subscription.

<Note>Your profile says whether plans are switched on where you are, and is where a subscription is started and managed (Stripe takes the payment; the app never sees a card). While Runlog is in preview, and on any copy you run yourself, everything above is open to everyone. Prices are on the hosted copy's pricing page, linked from the foot of this page.</Note>

## Running it yourself

Nothing here is a lock. The same build runs from a file on disk, from the public GitHub Pages copy, or from your own hosting, with no account and no plan. Selling a pack you sealed yourself, outside the catalog, is free too; the ledger is the service.

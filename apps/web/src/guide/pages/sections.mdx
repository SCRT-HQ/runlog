# The sections

The Designer's sections, in the order a pack is read.

- **What it is.** Title, id, version, author, description, and, for the marketplace, a category and tags. The license says whether copies may be shared.
- **The words.** What a run, a unit, a subject and closing a unit are called in this game; the app uses them everywhere.
- **Tables.** What the dice land on. An entry has its text and may grant a state, fire a trigger, carry points for moderated play, or need something optional.
- **Phases.** The flow every unit passes through: roll a table, declare the subject, do the work with a checklist, close.
- **Modes.** Ways to play: a length, a seed, seats for several players, a clock, a roster for moderated play, and what to leave out.

## Problems

**Problems** on the right lists anything that would stop the pack loading or looks like a mistake: a table whose ranges do not tile the die, a state nothing applies, a counter nothing changes. The same checks run from the terminal as [`validate`](#guide/cli-check).

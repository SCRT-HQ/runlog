# The sections

The Designer has six sections. They share one automatically saved draft, and you can move among them freely.

- **Overview.** What the pack is, what a person needs and the words the app uses for its parts. The less commonly changed id and version are in its fold.
- **Tables.** What the dice land on. An entry has its text and may grant a state, fire a trigger, carry points for moderated play, or need something optional.
- **Flow.** The phases every unit passes through: roll a table, declare the subject, do the work with a checklist, close.
- **Modes.** Ways to play: a length, a seed, seats for several players, a clock, a roster for moderated play, and what to leave out.
- **Test.** Every error and warning, links back to the fields that caused them, a rulebook preview and the pack's structure.
- **Publish.** A final look at the identifier and version, plus licensing, signing and sealing, generated documents, a downloadable file and a shareable link.

## Problems

Open **Test** for anything that would stop the pack loading or looks like a mistake: a table whose ranges do not tile the die, a state nothing applies, a counter nothing changes. A problem also appears beside its field, and choosing it in Test takes you there. The same checks run from the terminal as [`validate`](#guide/cli-check).

Pricing

The app is free and always will be. What costs money is what a server adds: a table other people can join, and a shop with a ledger.

Free

$0

  • Every pack, every mode, offline included
  • The Designer and the command line
  • Sync between your own devices
  • Play together on one device, moderated play included
  • Buy packs from the catalog

No account needed for most of it.

Plus

$4 a month, or $36 a year

  • Everything in Free
  • Invite people into your runs, as players or watchers, on their own devices
  • Races across devices, with a live leaderboard
  • Pop-out widgets for a stream: the scoreboard, the clock, the race

{{PLANS_NOTE}}

Servers

$9 a month, or $90 a year, per account

  • Runlog in a Discord server of yours, up to three
  • The bot hosts runs there, in a thread, on packs from your own shelf
  • People with the role you choose start runs; everyone in the server watches or plays
  • Every run also has a live link and stream widgets

In private beta while it is built: ask in the Discord to try it.

Publisher

5% of each sale

  • List packs in the catalog, free or priced
  • Paid through your own Stripe account
  • Sealed delivery, and a ledger: reissue a buyer's key, revoke one
  • Command-line publishing with API keys

Or 0% with hosted licensing, $9 a month or $90 a year, for publishers who sell often.

What never costs anything

Running the app yourself. Runlog is open source under the MIT license: the same build runs from a file on disk, from the public GitHub Pages copy, or from your own hosting, with no account and no fee. Selling a pack you sealed yourself, outside this catalog, is free too; only hosting the ledger for you is a service.

Cancelling

A subscription can be cancelled from your profile at any time and runs to the end of the period paid for. There is no cancellation fee.

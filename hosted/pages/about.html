About Runlog

A referee and run log for games played around the things you already do.

Runlog began as a way to turn a day at the wheel into a game: roll for the clay, roll for the form, roll for the twist, make the thing, log it. The engine underneath turned out to be general — a table is a table whether it says “porcelain” or “power-slide” — so it became a platform: any game of rounds, dice and consequences, written as a pack, played in the same app.

Open source, hosted here

The app, the engine, the schema and the command line are open source under the MIT license at github.com/SCRT-HQ/runlog.

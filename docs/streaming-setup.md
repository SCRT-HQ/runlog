# Streaming a run: OBS, Streamlabs, StreamElements, Streamer.bot

This walkthrough moved into the guide, one page per app, so it sits
beside the rest of what a streamer reads and is never out of step with
the app:

- [Streaming a run](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/streaming): the widgets.
- [The address that works on a stream](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/stream-address)
- [OBS Studio and Streamlabs](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/obs): the browser source, field by field, with sizes.
- [A dock for the controls](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/dock)
- [StreamElements](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/streamelements): the custom widget, with its four tabs.
- [Streamer.bot, Aitum and Lumia](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/streamer-bot): a dice alert and a `!score` command.
- [For a chat bot or your own tool](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/stream-tools)
- [When a widget does not follow the run](https://runlog.scrthq.com/play/guide/stream-troubles)

The numbers and the socket behind them stay documented here, in
[the stream API](stream-api.md), which is the contract a tool is built
against.
